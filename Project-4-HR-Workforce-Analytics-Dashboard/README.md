# HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

Designed and developed a Power BI dashboard to provide workforce and HR analytics through interactive reporting and executive-level KPIs. The dashboard enables HR managers and business leaders to monitor workforce trends, employee performance, attrition, compensation, training effectiveness, and organizational health to support strategic workforce planning.

---

## Business Problem

HR departments manage workforce data across multiple functions, making it difficult to identify employee trends, monitor retention, evaluate workforce performance, and support strategic decision-making using spreadsheets alone. This dashboard centralizes HR metrics into an interactive reporting solution that improves workforce visibility and enables data-driven HR decisions.

---

## Technology Stack

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Modeling
- HR Analytics
- Workforce Reporting
- Data Visualization

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Workforce Metrics

- Total Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Employee Satisfaction Score

### HR Performance

- Average Salary
- Training Hours
- Overtime Analysis

### Workforce Trends

- Department-wise Employee Distribution
- Work Mode Distribution
- Performance Rating Trends

---

## Business Value

This dashboard enables HR managers and business leaders to:

- Monitor workforce health using interactive HR KPIs
- Identify attrition trends and employee retention opportunities
- Analyze workforce distribution across departments
- Evaluate employee performance and training effectiveness
- Support strategic workforce planning through data-driven insights

---

## Sample Business Insights

- Employee attrition was concentrated within specific departments, highlighting opportunities for targeted retention initiatives.
- Performance ratings showed a positive relationship with employee training participation.
- Overtime analysis identified departments experiencing higher operational workloads.
- Workforce distribution and compensation trends supported more effective resource planning.
